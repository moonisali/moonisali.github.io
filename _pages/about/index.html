Moonis is a PhD candidate at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, advised by Anja Feldmann (Professor, MPI-INF) and Savvas Zannettou (Assistant Professor, TU Delft). His research sits at the intersection of Security and Computational Social Science, focusing on demystifying and understanding Online Harms.

Moonis is interested in creating a safer Internet experience for users across the globe with focus on vulnerable user groups like young people. To this end, he builds data-driven tools and analysis pipelines to demystify and understand Online Harms. His work ranges from quantifying Community-driven harms like self-harm discourse in Incel communities on Reddit to gauging the role of recommendation systems in spreading Algorithm-driven harms like recommendation of problematic content to young users on TikTok and Instagram.
