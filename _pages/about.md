Moonis is a PhD candidate at the [Max Planck Institute for Informatics](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/), advised by [Anja Feldmann](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/departments/inet/people/anja-feldmann) (Professor, MPI-INF) and [Savvas Zannettou](https://zsavvas.github.io/) (Assistant Professor, TU Delft). His research sits at the intersection of **Security** and **Computational Social Science**, focusing on demystifying and understanding online harms.

Moonis is interested in creating a safer Internet experience for users across the globe with focus on vulnerable users. To this end, he builds data-driven tools and analysis pipelines to demystify and understand online harms. His work ranges from Community-driven harms like self-harm discourse in Incel communities on Reddit) to  Algorithim-driven harms like recommendation-algorithms suggesting harmful content to young users.
